# Card Surface

## Scope and Mode

Persuade. This brief applies only to the `/card` route.

## Audience, Job, and Action

Visitors arrive from a shared link, most often on a phone. Within seconds they should recognize Danis, understand the technical range, and choose either the full portfolio or one verified public channel. The primary action is opening the full portfolio.

## Proof and Content

Use the confirmed name, "Creative Software Engineer" role, existing profile line, incumbent avatar artwork, and the GitHub, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram links already present in the portfolio. Do not add an email address, LinkedIn profile, full surname, availability claim, testimonial, metric, or experience number.

## Constraints

Mobile-first and safe-area aware, with full keyboard and screen-reader support. Keep touch targets at least 44px, preserve browser zoom, honor reduced motion, and avoid WebGL, audio, custom cursors, device blocking, or hidden desktop functionality. The route must load independently from the main Three.js bundle and remain usable as a narrow centered surface on larger screens.

## Direction and Memorable Moment

Tactile Pocket Portfolio. The page feels cut from the existing hand-drawn paper world, using real avatar artwork, graphite ink, local sketch type, and one blueprint-blue accent. The waving avatar enters from the right edge of the first viewport while the wordmark and primary action stay immediately legible. Social destinations read as tactile paper actions rather than generic link-in-bio tiles.

## Unresolved Decisions

The repository has no confirmed absolute production domain or dedicated `/card` social-preview image. Use the existing portfolio preview asset and relative URLs until those are supplied.
